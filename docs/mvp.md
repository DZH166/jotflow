# 小董随心录 MVP

## Product promise

Save a thought or useful piece of content in seconds, without naming or filing it, then find it later from imperfect memory.

## In scope

- Android and Windows capture entry points.
- Text, URL, image, and file records.
- Unified chronological inbox.
- Fuzzy full-text search over captured text and extracted metadata.
- Offline access to saved records.
- Account-based sync after the local capture loop is validated.

## Out of scope for the first slice

- AI auto-categorization.
- Complex folders, backlinks, and databases.
- Social sharing and collaboration.
- Active resurfacing or reminders.
- Guaranteed reproduction of authenticated, paywalled, or highly dynamic web pages.

## Success signal

The owner can save something from either phone or computer in under 10 seconds and find it again weeks later without remembering its original title.
